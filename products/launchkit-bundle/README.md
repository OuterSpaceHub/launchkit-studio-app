# LaunchKit Digital Product Bundle

This folder contains a starter bundle for the LaunchKit digital product you can sell on Gumroad, Shopify, or your own landing page.

## What is included

- `templates/landing-page-01.html` — a polished startup landing page template
- `copy/launchkit-positioning.md` — product positioning and messaging notes
- `copy/sales-copy.md` — a ready-to-use sales page pitch
- `assets/README.md` — how to package and sell the bundle

## Suggested product

Name: LaunchKit Assets Pack

Description: "A plug-and-play library of SaaS landing page sections, messaging prompts, and launch templates for startup founders."

## Suggested price

- Starter bundle: $29
- Complete pack: $79
- Bundle + updates: $129

## How to distribute

- Gumroad
- Shopify digital downloads
- Etsy
- Your own storefront with Stripe

## Quick packaging checklist

- Add product preview screenshots
- Add one short demo video
- Include a clear license and refund policy
- Provide instructions for edit/use
- Add a buyer onboarding email
